The Positive Impacts Of AI

What is AI ?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is technology that enables computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. It can learn from data, recognise patterns, solve problems and understand language. AI is used in many areas, including healthcare, education, business and entertainment, to improve efficiency, automate tasks and support decision-making.

The Three Main AI Companies

  • OpenAI – Creator of ChatGPT, developing advanced AI models and tools.
  • Google DeepMind – Develops Gemini and conducts research into advanced AI systems.
  • Anthropic – Creator of Claude, focusing on reliable and safe AI systems.
